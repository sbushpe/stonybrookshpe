Members Only · Design Preview

Member access.

Your SBU email is your key. Sign in to check into meetings, watch your attendance add up, and control exactly what sponsors get to see.

Sign in

We email you a one-time link to your inbox to confirm you're a student

The link works once and expires after 15 minutes. Send yourself a fresh one any time.

Create your account

Every field is required. We check your name and student ID against the chapter roster before the account goes live.

Those four fields are everything we store to get you in. Major, class year, and a resume are optional, and you add them later from the portal.
What you unlock

Your chapter, tracked.

The portal is the one place your membership actually lives. No sign-up sheets going missing, no asking an e-board member what your count is.

  • Check into meetings in about thirty seconds
  • A running attendance total that updates itself
  • Your resume in the sponsor book, only if you say so
Your student ID is used to match you to the roster and nothing else. It never appears in anything a sponsor can see.

How you get in.

01
Type it

Enter the SBU email you registered with. That address is the account, so use the same one every time.

02
Check mail

A one-time link lands in your inbox within a minute. It works once, then it is dead.

03
You're in

The link opens your portal and keeps you signed in on that device until you sign out.

Trouble getting the email? Check spam, then tell any e-board member at the next general body meeting and we will sort it out.
