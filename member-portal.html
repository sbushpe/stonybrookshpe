How it counts

One sheet, one total.

Every check-in lands on the event's attendance sheet: your name, SBU email, and student ID. A master roster keeps the running total per member, so your count is always current and never retyped by hand.

No ID on you? Check in with your SBU email and the e-board verifies it after the meeting. It shows as pending until then.

Attendance records live in access-controlled storage. Only the e-board can see the roster, and sponsors only ever see totals.

Where it goes

Locked down, by design.

Your file uploads over an encrypted connection and lives in a private, access-controlled bucket. It is never posted publicly and never sent over open links.

Only sponsors at the qualifying tier can open the resume book, every download is logged, and access ends when a sponsorship does.

Removing your file or flipping the toggle off takes effect right away, no questions asked.

Why we ask

Counted together, never alone.

Sponsors back this chapter because of who we are: first-gen students, Pell recipients, students from every borough and beyond. Your answers make that story visible in aggregate.

Individual answers are never shown to anyone outside the e-board. Sponsors see one dashboard for the whole chapter, with no way to trace a number back to a person.

You can change or clear any answer whenever you want, and "prefer not to say" is always an option.
