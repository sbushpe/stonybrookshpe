Why it matters

Meet them before the fair.

Chapters like ours are a direct line to Hispanic and underrepresented students in engineering, computer science, and applied math, years before they hit the open market.

Gold partners get the resume book, the numbers behind the chapter, and a seat in the room. Bronze and Silver keep the relationship warm until you're ready to go deeper.

Questions about invoicing or benefits? Your chapter liaison answers within two business days.
